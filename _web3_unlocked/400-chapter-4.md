We’ve covered the "why" and the "how" of Web3's building blocks. Now, we get to the exciting part: seeing it in action. Where is the code meeting the real world? This chapter is a tour of the Web3 landscape, starting with the applications you can use today and journeying out to the bleeding edge of what’s next.

Our first stop is a paradox. For a world built on decentralization, its most-used product is often anything but: the **stablecoin**. For years, Bitcoin dominated crypto trading, but since the early 2020s, stablecoins have taken over[^1]. They solve the market's crippling volatility but often by relying on the very centralized institutions Web3 aims to disrupt. This tension between utility and ideology is one of the most important stories in Web3 today, and time will tell if decentralized alternatives can truly take hold.

From there, we explore why blockchain’s first and most natural target was **finance**. Money has been digital for decades, and any system built on bits and bytes is ripe for a software-based revolution. **Decentralized Finance (DeFi)** had its breakout moment in 2020 and has since grown into the most mature and battle-tested corner of the Web3 ecosystem.

Next, we dive into the world of **NFTs**. After a meteoric rise in 2021, the initial hype cooled, forcing a deeper conversation. What is an NFT beyond a digital collectible? We’ll peel back the layers to see how they fundamentally differ from cryptocurrencies and, more importantly, what new kinds of value—from provenance to property rights—they can bring to any artifact.

From finance, we turn to our online lives. Unlike finance, which only adopted digital, **social media** was *born* digital. We’ll explore how Web3 is reimagining social platforms, which leads directly to one of the most vital questions of our time: who are you online? A truly sovereign **digital identity**, controlled by you, is a cornerstone of the Web3 vision.

But Web3 isn't just about software; it has to run on hardware. This brings us to **Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Networks (DePIN)**. It’s an often-overlooked but critical frontier: think decentralized file storage, wireless networks, or even energy grids. Building in the physical world requires immense capital and patience. While DePIN represents a long-term vision, it has rapidly emerged as one of Web3's most active sectors and is essential for true decentralization.

Finally, we’ll venture to the emerging frontiers where the most radical ideas are just beginning to sprout. We’ll see how new models like **Decentralized Science (DeSci)** are being created to fund research and how **Regenerative Finance (ReFi)** aims to build regenerative economies. Then, we’ll tackle one of the most powerful intersections in tech today: the convergence of **Web3 and Artificial Intelligence**.

Throughout this tour, we'll ground these concepts in real-world projects, showing you the teams and ideas building this future as we speak.

[^1]: Although Bitcoin remains the top cryptocurrency by market cap, stablecoins have dominated crypto trading volume since the early 2020s, making up around 40% of exchange volume and with USDT and USDC comprising nearly 90% of stablecoin market capitalization.
