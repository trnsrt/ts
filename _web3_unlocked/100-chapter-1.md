Web3 didn't appear out of thin air—it emerged [gradually](https://www.internetsociety.org/internet/history-internet/brief-history-internet/) from earlier versions of the internet. Understanding Web3 requires looking closely at the worlds that shaped it: Web 1.0 and Web 2.0.
