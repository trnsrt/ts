Our journey through the architecture of Web3 has brought us to the final, most important question: What happens next?

Depending on when you’re reading this, the hype around Web3 might feel like the dawn of a new age or a solution in search of a problem. This whiplash is normal for a technology this new. Outside of finance, Web3 is still finding its killer apps, which is why sentiment can swing from "world-changing" to "worthless" overnight.

The truth, as always, isn't in the headlines but in the space between the extremes.

Rather than get caught up in market speculation, we’re going to focus on the fundamental technological shifts on the horizon. The projects we'll explore are the foundational layers—the bedrock upon which the future will be built.

To map out what's coming, we’ll look at the road ahead in two stages. First, we'll examine the current critical period (2025-2026), which is seeing foundational technologies mature at a rapid pace. Then, we’ll look further out, to the long-term evolution between 2026 and 2030.
