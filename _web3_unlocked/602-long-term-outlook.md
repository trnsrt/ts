Peering into the long-term future is notoriously difficult. The technology landscape shifts so fast that today's bold prediction can be tomorrow's punchline. Still, by tracing the trajectory of today's most powerful trends, we can outline a sketch of what's to come:

- By 2026, the market’s center of gravity will shift from individual enthusiasts to institutional capital.
- CeDeFi will be the common model for financial services. You'll use your favorite banking app, but its most innovative products will run on DeFi protocols in the background, making decentralized finance both invisible and mainstream.
- Tokenization will expand beyond securities into the physical world, as legal frameworks and new infrastructure unlock the ability to represent real-world assets on-chain.
- The wallet will become the only interface that matters. Users won’t know or care which network they’re on; the wallet will manage risk and use 'intents' to execute their goals seamlessly in the background.
- A true breakthrough in the metaverse will arrive, but only after the hardware challenges are solved—a reminder that progress with atoms is always slower than progress with bits.
- Core infrastructure will be upgraded to become quantum-resistant, a necessary evolution to secure the network against future threats.
- Successful DAO governance models will mature, becoming powerful vehicles for funding ambitious projects in decentralized science (DeSci) and physical infrastructure (DePIN). Don't be surprised to one day see a DAO rank among the world's largest institutions.
- By 2028, the conversation will be dominated by one thing: AI. The central conflict will be the hegemony of Big Tech versus a new wave of decentralized, Web3-native AI projects.
- The fusion of AI and blockchain technology will create entirely new industries we can't yet imagine.
- The world will have [one billion Web3 users](https://a16zcrypto.com/posts/article/state-of-crypto-report-2024/) by 2030, a number that could be multiplied by AI agents acting as network participants.
- Autonomous AI agents will become the primary users of Web3. Humans may initiate only 10% of the network's transactions; the rest will be executed 24/7 by tireless agents whose only need is electricity.
